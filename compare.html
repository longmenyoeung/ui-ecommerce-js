Plain Striola shirts are casual short-sleeve garments featuring a distinctive striped pattern and a chest pocket. These shirts are designed for everyday wear, offering a blend of comfort and style. They are typically made from materials like polyester, ensuring breathability and durability.

Tangoli presents the Chen Frills Cardigan, a short cardigan adorned with frills all around. Made from a Parisian dead-stock fabric—a beautiful cotton viscose blend—it closes with a ribbon at the waist. This high-hip-length cardigan features long sleeves and is available in sizes 34 to 44.

A Henley shirt is a versatile and classic garment that is similar to a polo shirt but without a collar. It typically features a round neckline and a placket with a few buttons (usually 2 to 5).
